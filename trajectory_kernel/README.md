# Trajectory–Kernel Programme

This directory contains the new analytic programme for understanding positive
$SO(10)$ lifts of the Bars cumulative spectrum. It begins after the completed
repair and audit phase recorded in the root `STATUS.md`.

## Objective

Starting from the baseline-free HFT $SO(9)$ spectrum, investigate whether the
restriction-kernel corrections required for positive $SO(10)$ lifts are
organised by subleading Regge trajectories.

The basic $SO(9)$ labels are written

$$
[\ell,m,r,s]_9.
$$

A Regge column fixes $(m,r,s)$ and studies the joint shift of oscillator level
and leading label $\ell$. Its shift-invariant reduced depth is
$\delta=n-\ell-3m$, the coordinate natural in the HFT support. The programme
asks whether mixed-parent kernel corrections can be generated from finitely
many boundary seeds under this shift.

  `cumulative_regge_uplift.py`, `scalar_boundary_functional.py`,
  `ddf_koszul_audit.py`, `gamma_symbol_complex.py` and
  `milp_certification.py` are also derivation
  modules. They must not import any protected baseline file.
- `verify_against_baseline.py` is the only module in this directory permitted
  to import `massive_SO10.py` or another protected baseline.
- Comparisons must occur only after a candidate rule has been constructed from
  baseline-free input.
- A failed recurrence, proliferating kernel basis, or infeasible positivity
  system is a result and must be reported rather than patched with known data.

The inherited root modules remain the trusted inputs:

- `hft_so9.py`: independent analytic $SO(9)$ spectrum;
- `finite_cross_section.py`: exact triangular inverse on chosen sections;
- `search_finite_cross_sections.py`: finite one-parent obstruction;
- `plethystic_series.py`: formal character-series operations.

## Milestones

1. **TK1 — Regge columns (complete through level 10):** Extract the multiplicity functions
   $a_{\ell,m,r,s}(n)$ from the HFT-derived $SO(9)$ characters and identify the
   exact stable-shift domain.
2. **TK2 — Shift identities (complete):** Verify which branching columns and
   kernel relations are covariant under $\ell\mapsto\ell+1$. The result is an
   exact boundary-corrected shift, not raw covariance.
3. **TK3 — Positive correction fibres (complete through level 10):** Express
   all nonnegative lifts as a reference section plus integral trajectory-kernel
   corrections, without baseline input.
4. **TK4 — Analytic trajectory selection and anomaly (in progress):** Simple
   corrected-shift extremal rules are cutoff-stable through level 11 but do not
   select a unique or rule-independent lift. TK4b finds the first exact
   numerator anomaly at level 2. TK4c proves that its class modulo
   $Z_Q\ker(\operatorname{Res})$ is nonzero and that one shifted seed does not
   generate the later classes in the certified window. TK4d verifies the
   positive intrinsic closed supermultiplet and shows that tensoring the
   current virtual chiral uplifts does not reproduce it or restore level-2
   divisibility.
5. **TK5 — Chamber transport and residual sources:** TK5a verifies the
   empirical GMSZ support polytope and its chamber-restricted $\ell$ and $m$
   multiplicity shifts through level 11. TK5b isolates the complementary core,
   shows that the finite kernel lattice is generated by corrected shifts of
   two nontransported objects, and finds positive level-11 paths whose
   coordinate sources occur only at residual-core positions. TK5c performs
   GLPK-backed minimization with exact primal-witness checks: the level-11
   minimum reported by the solver has seven events and charge 20, six distinct
   witnesses survive, and no witness is minimal at every cutoff from 7 through
   11. Optimality and exhaustion remain solver-backed.
6. **TK6a — Principal restriction-kernel ideal (complete through the
   level-11 finite basis):** The global representation-ring kernel is generated
   by $J=16_+-16_-$. All eleven natural finite kernel generators are $J$ times
   positive integral characters and obey simple partial-sum formulas in the
   tested window.
7. **TK6b.1 — Finite positive restriction semigroup (complete through the
   level-11 closure):** The $73\times84$ packet matrix is not totally
   unimodular, but its 84 columns are exactly the Hilbert basis of its rational
   cone. The finite semigroup is saturated and has 2,508 irredundant facets.
   All cumulative HFT targets through level 11 satisfy them.
8. **TK6b.2 — Facet-circuit classification (finite classification complete):**
   Direct Dynkin-translation templates proliferate, but the 2,508 level-11
   facets reduce to 40 positive-circuit types of 29 Gale row types. The Gale
   rows obey one compact $A/B/C$ template, and every facet becomes a zero-one
   subset-sum inequality in triangular section coordinates. The circuit list
   itself has not stabilized, so the all-orders proof remains open.
9. **TK6b.3 — Boundary shells (local all-split theorem complete; first
   new-family horizon audited):** The $A/B/C$ row rule is proved for arbitrary
   lengths of those chains, but two new kernel families enter the HFT closure
   at level 12. Every consecutive parent split obeys a universal six-term
   identity, whose telescoping sum gives the $4k+2$-term natural relation for
   the $k$th parent. The revised all-level object is a growing all-split
   collision hypergraph.
10. **TK6b.4 — Weighted positive circuits (finite classification through HFT
    level 13):** Repeated Gale rows admit an exact circuit compression, and
    primitive circuits give weighted liftability inequalities. Unit
    coefficients persist through level 12 but fail at level 13. The cumulative
    targets through level 13 satisfy all weighted inequalities and have exact
    positive integral packet witnesses; all-orders control remains open.
11. **TK6b.5 — Local HFT weighted inequalities (analytic reduction
    complete):** The all-chiral triangular inverse is the three-fold local
    Möbius difference of the $B_4$ multiplicity function. Every weighted
    circuit therefore pulls back to an explicit finite signed HFT
    multiplicity inequality. The operator commutes exactly with Dynkin-label
    translations and cumulative level sums. This supplies the required HFT
    bridge, but a recursive circuit classification, all-orders positivity and
    integral saturation remain open.
12. **TK6b.5b/c — Prefix-shell duality and Regge tails:** Eliminating each
    unique alternative pivot gives a uniform cone $y\geq0$, $Ly\geq0$. The
    full infinite operator has no nonzero finitely supported positive circuit,
    so finite facets are moving boundary-tail objects. Universal interval
    tails telescope to at most eight HFT endpoints, while mixed pullbacks can
    still grow. Exact HFT symmetric-vector factorization shows every tested
    eligible $\ell$ shift holds term by term in the virtual remainder through
    level 13. An all-degree remainder-support theorem is the next analytic
    target.
13. **TK6b.5d — Stable $B_n$ Pieri translation:** A Racah--Speiser argument
    proves the consecutive symmetric-power translation for arbitrary
    tensorial or spinorial weights once the source target lies beyond the
    remainder's first orthogonal coordinate. All 524 active HFT remainder
    couplings in the 185 eligible shifts through level 13 satisfy this exact
    hypothesis. A sharper exterior-cost inequality excludes every unstable
    product term through total degree 12; TK6B.5f subsequently proves the
    required support statement at every degree.
14. **TK6b.5e — Finite Pieri hazard and HFT filtered core:** The unstable
    harmonic search has the analytic cutoff $k<2\mu_1$, and its exact source
    threshold is a wall-piecewise maximum of seven affine bounds. The formula
    is proved for tensorial and spinorial weights. It reduces all-orders
    stable-range coverage to the HFT support theorem
    $[V_\mu]F_d\neq0\Rightarrow K(\mu)\leq d$. Exact oscillator identities
    isolate this as filtered divisibility by $Z_Q$; TK6B.5f proves the
    implication beyond the formerly verified degree-12 window.
15. **TK6b.5f — Analytic HFT support:** Green--Schwarz triality gives an
    exact telescoping formula for the virtual remainder. Weyl/Dirac folding
    does not raise the seven-wall degree, uniform vector/spinor estimates
    close the infinite mode tail, and the remaining 323-record low-mode
    certificate has no failures. Hence
    $[V_\mu]F_d\neq0\Rightarrow d\geq K(\mu)$ at every degree. The remaining
    Bars problem is the global recursive circuit/packetization theorem, not
    further HFT level testing.
16. **TK6b.6 — Cumulative Regge uplift and primitive scalar target:** The
    Bars cumulative factor completes the HFT vector tower to a restricted
    $D_5$ tower. A two-seed correction is positive through level 13, and its
    scalar tail reduces at all orders to
    $\pi_3(N)\geq\pi_4(N-1)$. The transverse DDF creators yield the natural
    Koszul map, but its raw middle term has
    $\ker d_4=\operatorname{im}d_5$. The corrected source is the primitive
    quotient $K_4/\operatorname{im}d_5$. The cumulative character has the
    exact factorization
    $Z_m/(1-q)=\operatorname{PE}(q9)\widehat G$, with canonical $B_4$ core
    $\widehat G$ positive through degree 20. Relative Dirac descent both
    reconstructs this core from the transverse quotient and produces five
    gamma-traceless modules. Their explicit projected-wedge maps form an
    exact polynomial free resolution with positive cokernel
    $\bigoplus_{n\geq0}[n,0,3,0]_{D_4}$. The null fibre has nonzero homology,
    so vanishing of the truncated $\operatorname{Tor}_1$ is neither true nor
    the correct target; the full five-term derived tensor product must be
    retained. The orthosymplectic operator
    $Q^1=\gamma\cdot\alpha_{-1}/\sqrt2$ instead organizes the trace
    multiplicity module and obeys $\{Q^1,Q^1\}=T^{11}$; it is not the
    adjacent projected-wedge map. Their local combination is now explicit on
    the full spinor-form triangle: $\Gamma E+E\Gamma=C$, the trace Laplacians
    have spectra $(8)$, $(6,14)$, $(4,10,18)$ and $(2,6,12,20)$, and the
    projected defect is exactly $-2P'E(\Gamma G)^{-1}CI$. Constructing this
    filtered triangle on the mass-graded DDF module, proving compatibility
    with universal-supermultiplet division, and establishing homology
    concentration remain open. The all-orders positivity and actual
    regular-sequence realization of $\widehat G$ are parallel open tasks. An
    exact order-eight Weyl recurrence further reduces the scalar functional
    on $\widehat G$ to the three principal hook trajectories $(k)$, $(k,1)$
    and $(k,1,1)$. Contraction plus exterior multiplication injects the middle
    hook into the two outer hooks at every first-row length, leaving the
    module-level lift as the direct orthosymplectic alternative. Positive
    relative-Dirac orientation upgrades this to an injective free
    $\operatorname{Sym}(8_v)$ symbol. The complete resolution moves through
    the cumulative-core pairing and yields
    $(1-q)\Phi=(1+q)H_4-(1+q^2)B_\Gamma$, where $B_\Gamma$ is a positive pairing whenever
    the core is positive. Correct outer orientation makes its prefix kernel
    exactly $\mathfrak D^+(R_{n+1}-R_n)$: it restricts positively to the
    single `Spin(7)` shell $[n,0,3]$ but remains virtual in `Spin(8)`. Thus
    the resolution explains, rather than bypasses, the earlier three-hook
    obstruction. A coefficientwise Regge
    injection is refuted by many negative first-row differences, so the
    concrete target is a diagonal filtered complex mixing core degree and
    first-row degree, not a level-raising map inferred from dimensions alone.
