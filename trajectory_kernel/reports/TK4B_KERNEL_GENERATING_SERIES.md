# TK4b — Kernel-Valued Source Series and HFT Numerator Compatibility

**Status:** completed baseline-free experiment; both strict proposals encounter
early obstructions

**Provenance:** baseline-free through HFT level 11

**Last updated:** 23 September 2026

**Later refinement:** TK4c proves that the level-2 remainder represents a
nonzero class modulo $Z_Q^{10}\ker(\operatorname{Res})$. Thus the exact
quotient is obstructed even if arbitrary virtual, rather than only positive,
kernel corrections are allowed. See `TK4C_KERNEL_ANOMALY.md`.

## Result

TK4b tests two stronger analytic ideas:

1. all kernel corrections are generated by finitely many $\ell=0$ boundary
   sources under the exact corrected shift;
2. the independently lifted HFT numerator selects the positive cumulative
   lifts through exact division by $Z_Q^{10}$.

Neither statement is true as written.

- Boundary-only kernel generation first fails at level 9.
- Exact numerator compatibility with positive cumulative lifts first fails at
  level 2, before any adjustable kernel coordinate is present.

The failures are exact algebraic obstructions, not failed peeling algorithms.
They refine rather than contradict the useful trajectory organization found in
TK2--TK4a.

## 1. Kernel-valued source transform

For a trajectory family $F$, let $z_F(n,\ell)$ be the coefficient of its
kernel generator at level $n$. Define

$$
s_F(n,0)=z_F(n,0),
$$

and, for $\ell>0$,

$$
s_F(n,\ell)=z_F(n,\ell)-z_F(n-1,\ell-1).
$$

If

$$
C_F(q,t)=\sum_{n,\ell}z_F(n,\ell)q^nt^\ell,
$$

then the source series is

$$
S_F(q,t)=(1-qt)C_F(q,t).
$$

Here $C_F$ is the coordinate series and $S_F$ is the source series. The
notation deliberately avoids $Z_F$, which already denotes the HFT fermionic
partition function elsewhere in the project. Terms with $\ell=0$ are boundary
sources. Terms with $\ell>0$ measure genuine
failure of transport by the corrected shift. The transform is exactly
invertible; the implementation verifies reconstruction of every analyzed
coordinate path.

## 2. Boundary-only generation is obstructed

Exact integer optimization gives the minimum possible total absolute interior
source charge through each horizon:

| Horizon | Minimum interior-source charge |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1--8 | 0 |
| 9 | 1 |
| 10 | 3 |
| 11 | 4 |

Thus all positive corrections can be propagated from boundary data through
level 8, but at level 9 at least one positive-$\ell$ source is unavoidable.
The required bulk-source charge then grows in the next two levels.

For one transport-first representative through level 11, the source series has
ten nonzero monomials: seven boundary terms of total charge 21 and three
interior terms of total charge 4. Other intrinsic priorities redistribute this
complexity:

| Representative rule | Source terms | Total charge | Boundary terms/charge | Interior terms/charge |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Transport first | 10 | 25 | 7 / 21 | 3 / 4 |
| Boundary first | 9 | 20 | 3 / 4 | 6 / 16 |
| Total deviation | 12 | 20 | 7 / 10 | 5 / 10 |

No one of these finite source polynomials is distinguished by the present
representation-theoretic data. In particular, “finite boundary seeds” must be
weakened to allow structured interior sources.

## 3. Exact HFT numerator test

Let $N_n^{10}$ be the independently constructed virtual $SO(10)$ HFT numerator
and let $L_n$ be a positive cumulative lift of $R_n^9$. Exact direct division
would require

$$
N_n^{10}=Z_Q^{10}(L_n-L_{n-1}).
$$

This equation, positivity of every $L_n$, integrality and exact restriction
were imposed simultaneously as a finite integer feasibility problem. It is
feasible at level 1 and infeasible upon adding level 2.

At these levels the positive cumulative lifts are still fixed:

$$
L_1=[0,0,0,0,0]_{10},
$$

$$
L_2=[1,0,0,0,0]_{10}.
$$

No trajectory-kernel coordinate is yet available to modify them. The level-2
remainder is

$$
\mathcal R_2=-2[0,0,0,0,0]+[0,0,0,0,2]+[0,0,0,2,0]
-2[0,0,0,1,1]+2[0,0,1,0,0]-2[0,1,0,0,0]
+2[1,0,0,0,0].
$$

Its dimension is zero and its restriction to $SO(9)$ vanishes exactly. Writing
$A_0$ for the 126/210 kernel generator and

$$
J_{126}=[0,0,0,2,0]-[0,0,0,0,2],
$$

for the outer-chirality difference, the remainder has the compact form

$$
\mathcal R_2=-2A_0+J_{126}.
$$

This identity is checked directly by the implementation. It shows that the
HFT numerator obstruction already lives in the same restriction-kernel algebra
that later controls positive lifts, but it appears before the corresponding
positive correction direction is available.

## 4. Interpretation

TK4b rules out two overly simple versions of the hoped-for principle:

- positive lifts are not generated solely by propagating $\ell=0$ boundary
  sources;
- the virtual $SO(10)$ HFT numerator is not exactly $Z_Q^{10}$ times the
  consecutive differences of positive cumulative lifts.

Therefore the M4 difficulty is not merely that highest-weight peeling chose a
bad order. There is an actual nonzero restriction-kernel remainder already at
level 2.

The useful surviving possibility is a **kernel-anomalous quotient**

$$
N_n^{10}=Z_Q^{10}(L_n-L_{n-1})+\mathcal K_n,
$$

where $\mathcal K_n$ is a structured, dimension-zero restriction-kernel series.
The level-2 term is fixed above. A viable next programme would need to derive
$\mathcal K(q)$ from a symmetry, chirality or cohomological principle; simply
minimizing its coefficient norm would repeat the arbitrariness exposed in
TK4a.

## Limitations

- The source-complexity comparisons concern representatives of non-unique
  optimized fibres.
- The absence of new fixed-tail families has only been checked through level
  11.
- The kernel-anomaly equation is currently a reformulation of the obstruction,
  not yet a derivation of the positive lift.
